How to Clean Shower Mold in Colton, CA, and the Signs It Is a Leak

Surface spots on caulk are a housekeeping job. Spots that return, a soft wall or a stain on the ceiling below are a plumbing and drying job.

The routine that works for surface mold on caulk and grout

If the spots sit on the surface of caulk, grout or a glazed tub wall, you can usually clean them yourself. Open the bathroom door, run the exhaust fan, and use soap or dish detergent, warm water and a stiff brush. Scrub, rinse well and dry the surface with a towel.

Work one product at a time. Do not combine bleach with ammonia-based cleaners or other chemicals, because the fumes can injure you. Wear gloves and keep the room ventilated, and stop if your eyes or throat react.

Dark spots that remain deep in old caulk usually mean the caulk is finished. Cut it out, let the joint dry completely, and apply fresh mildew-resistant caulk. A bead that goes on a damp joint traps moisture behind it and starts the cycle again.

Three habits that slow regrowth

  • Run the exhaust fan during the shower and for a while afterward, so humid air leaves the room instead of settling on the grout.
  • Squeegee or towel the walls and glass after use. Standing droplets are what keep caulk lines damp.
  • Leave the shower curtain spread out, not bunched, so the liner dries.

These habits reduce regrowth but cannot promise it will not return. If spots reappear in the same corner within a few weeks despite good habits, treat that as a clue about moisture and not about cleaning technique.

Signs the problem is behind the wall

Surface mold sits where water splashes. A leak sits where water travels. The difference shows up in the wall, not in the caulk.

A shower pan leak is a plumbing repair first. After the plumber stops the water, a crew dries the structure and removes anything that cannot be saved. When water has run for a while, emergency water removal is the right call if you can see it pooling or dripping.

What a professional visit looks like

The crew starts with a moisture meter on the wall around the shower and the room below. A thermal imaging camera shows temperature differences that suggest wet material behind finished tile or drywall. Readings go on a map so the affected area is clear.

Where the wall is wet, the finished surface is opened far enough to reach dry framing and the wet material comes out. Air movers and a dehumidifier then dry the cavity. Once readings are normal, a contractor rebuilds the wall and sets new tile.

That rebuilding stage is water damage repair. Mold on the back of the board is handled under mold remediation, and the written scope names what is removed and how the work area is sealed.

Hard questions to ask yourself before reaching for the spray

Has the area been wet for longer than a few days? Is any material soft, swollen or discolored beyond the caulk line? Is there a smell that comes back an hour after cleaning? If any answer is yes, the cleaning bottle is the wrong tool.

Renters should photograph the spots and the surrounding wall, note the dates and report in writing to the landlord. Homeowners should photograph too, then read their policy. A leak that starts suddenly is more likely to be covered than seepage that has run for months, so your insurer will want a clear timeline.

A Colton water note

According to the City of Colton water division page, Colton's drinking water is city-run and drawn entirely from deep wells, with 10 wells, 6 reservoirs and more than 120 miles of pipe. Water, sewer and electric service are all billed by the City of Colton. If your bill jumps with no change in use, the utility is the first call to learn how to read your meter.

The meter check is simple. Turn off every fixture, look at the meter's leak indicator or dial, and see whether it still moves. A moving dial points to a leak somewhere between the meter and the house, including inside a wall.

If a shower wall is rebuilt in Colton, the Building and Safety Division is the office to ask about a permit, since the answer depends on scope. In neighboring Loma Linda, the city's own Public Works handles water and sewer, so confirm which utility serves a property outside Colton. Rialto customers should check their bill to see their provider.

Frequently asked questions

Wipe the walls and glass after most showers and scrub the caulk line monthly. How often depends on ventilation, because a shower with a strong exhaust fan dries faster than one in a windowless room with a weak fan.

Painting hides the stain but does not remove wet material. A wall that stays damp lifts the paint again. Clean and dry the surface first, and bring in a professional if the wall is soft or smells.

Shut off every fixture and appliance, then look at the meter's leak indicator or sweep dial. If it keeps moving, water is escaping somewhere. The City of Colton utility can explain where the meter sits on your property.

It is an emergency when water is actively running into a wall or ceiling, for example a pan or valve leak. Shut off water to the fixture and call the 24/7 line. Surface spots on caulk are not an emergency.
