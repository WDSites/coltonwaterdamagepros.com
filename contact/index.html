How to reach us

Call (909) 505-3870 any time, day or night. A coordinator answers, takes the address and the water source, and explains the next step. A crew can often come out the same day, depending on availability.

Hours

24/7 dispatch. There is no form or online booking, because a call is faster.

What to have ready when you call

  • The address and the nearest cross street.
  • Where the water is coming from, and whether it has been stopped.
  • Which rooms are wet, and whether water has reached ceilings or floors below.
  • Whether you have shut off the main water valve or any power.
  • Your insurer's name, if you plan to file a claim, since the crew can document the loss for them.

If anything electrical or gas related is involved, phone the utility or 911 first, then call us. Photos taken before you move anything help the assessment and the claim.
