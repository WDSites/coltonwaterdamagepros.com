About Colton Water Damage Pros

A call-only 24/7 dispatch line for water damage and mold work in Colton, CA and the towns around it.

☎ Call (909) 505-3870

What we do

Colton Water Damage Pros answers the emergency line and dispatches licensed and insured crews to water damage and mold jobs in Colton, CA. The work covers emergency water removal, structural drying, cleanup, repair, flood response and mold remediation.

This site gives no names, licence numbers or years in business because the dispatched crews vary by job. What stays constant is the process: a call, an assessment, a scope and price you hear first, and documentation of the loss.

The business address is 1200-1330 E Washington St, Colton, CA 92324, and the line is open around the clock. Colton's own utilities run the city's water, sewer and electric service, so a main-line failure in the street is a call to the city, not to us.

Who answers the phone

A coordinator takes the call at any hour, including nights, weekends and holidays. Their job is to log the address, learn where the water is coming from and decide which kind of crew fits, whether that is extraction, drying, cleanup or a mold assessment.

The coordinator does not diagnose by guesswork and does not quote a final price over the phone. A fair number depends on what the crew finds once they can put a moisture meter on the wall, and the coordinator will say so plainly.

Same-day response is offered when crews are free. We make no promise about an arrival window, because traffic on the I-10 and I-215 corridors, other emergencies and the size of the loss all change the schedule.

What to have ready when you call

  • The street address and the nearest cross street, so the coordinator can place you quickly.
  • Where the water started, if you know, and whether it has been shut off at a fixture or the main valve.
  • Which rooms and floors are wet, and whether any ceiling is sagging or any outlet sits in or near the water.
  • Whether the water looks clean, grey or contaminated, since sewage and storm runoff change the safety steps.
  • Your insurer's name and your policy number, if you want crews to start the paperwork trail early.

If an outlet, panel or appliance is touching water, stay out of the room and call the electric utility or 911 before you call us. Cut power at the panel only if the panel is dry and you can reach it without standing in water.

How scope and price are explained

Before equipment goes in, the crew lead walks the affected area with you and says what they see. They name the water category, the materials that are wet, the rooms involved and the equipment they plan to run, such as air movers, dehumidifiers and extraction units.

Price follows from that list. Square footage, the kind of flooring and wall material, how many days of drying the readings call for and how much rebuild you want afterward are the drivers. We do not post price ranges here, because a number without a look at your house would be a guess.

Ask for the scope in writing if you plan to send it to your insurer. Photos and daily moisture readings are part of the record, and they matter more to an adjuster than a verbal summary.

Standards and limits

S500 from ANSI and the IICRC is the published standard for restoring water-damaged buildings, and S520 does the same for mold remediation. We name them so you can ask any company, including this one, whether its crews work to them. We do not claim a firm certification on this site.

We do not handle fire damage, and we do not give legal or insurance advice. For insurance questions, check your policy and ask your insurer. If the real fix for your problem is waterproofing, plumbing or grading, we will say so.

We also make no health claims about mold. California does not license mold assessors or remediators, so it is worth asking any contractor what training and process they bring. Questions about symptoms belong with your physician.

What we do not claim is just as important as what we do. There are no reviews, ratings, awards or job counts on this site, because none are published here. We would rather you judge us by how the call and the first visit go.
